# Fictional Relationship Review Playbook

This playbook is invented and uses no confidential sales or servicing rules.

## Meeting preparation

Before a customer review, the RM confirms the reporting period and examines deposit and loan trends, product holdings, service issues, upcoming maturities, profitability components, recent contact notes, and unresolved requests. Balances are treated as snapshots and revenue or expense measures as period flows.

## Opportunity review

A product gap is a conversation prompt, not a sales recommendation. The RM connects a potential product to an observed need, validates that need with the customer, confirms eligibility and suitability, and involves the appropriate product specialist. The RM records why an opportunity was pursued, deferred, or dismissed.

## Retention review

For sustained deposit decline, the RM considers seasonality, business performance, major purchases, debt service, funds moved to another institution, and service dissatisfaction. A customer conversation should use neutral questions and must not state that analytics proved attrition.

## Human accountability

AI-generated summaries and retrieved guidance are drafts. The RM verifies customer facts, source version, policy applicability, and required approvals. The RM remains accountable for the final interpretation and any customer communication.
