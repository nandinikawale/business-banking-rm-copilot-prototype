# Fictional Business Customer Due Diligence Procedure

This is a simplified fictional procedure for a software portfolio. It is not legal or compliance guidance.

## Business and ownership information

The illustrative onboarding file records the legal entity name, formation details, physical and mailing addresses, tax identifier, nature and purpose of the business, expected account activity, authorized signers, and beneficial ownership information required by the fictional procedure. Information is obtained from approved sources and validated by authorized staff.

## Ongoing review

An RM does not overwrite conflicting customer information without review. Changes in ownership, business activity, address, expected transactions, or control persons are routed through the approved maintenance process. Unusual activity is not investigated with the customer when doing so would conflict with escalation instructions.

## Escalation

Missing ownership information, identity inconsistencies, unexplained high-risk activity, sanctions or screening alerts, and requests to bypass controls require escalation to the appropriate compliance or operations team. The assistant must not resolve alerts, determine suspicious activity, or advise a customer about confidential monitoring.
