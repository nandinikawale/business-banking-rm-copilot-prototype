# Fictional Business Line of Credit Guide

This document is invented for a portfolio demonstration. It is not a bank policy.

## Purpose and preliminary eligibility

A revolving business line of credit is intended to support recurring short-term working-capital needs such as timing differences between receivables and operating expenses. It is not intended to finance owner distributions, speculative investments, or a permanent operating loss. The RM records the stated purpose, requested amount, expected repayment source, and anticipated usage pattern before referral to credit.

The fictional preliminary screen considers time in business, operating history, current obligations, adverse events, and whether the request is consistent with demonstrated cash flow. Passing a screen is not an approval. Credit authority remains with an authorized credit officer.

## Documentation checklist

For an established business, the illustrative package includes two fiscal years of business financial statements or tax returns, a current interim income statement and balance sheet, a business debt schedule, recent business bank statements, ownership information, and authorization for applicable credit inquiries. Guarantor information may be requested under the fictional credit process.

The RM checks document dates, entity names, accounting periods, internal consistency, and material differences from information already on file. Missing, stale, or contradictory information is documented and escalated rather than inferred.

## Review and decision boundaries

The RM may explain product mechanics and collect information but does not promise pricing, structure, or approval. Credit evaluates repayment capacity, leverage, liquidity, guarantor support, collateral when applicable, and policy exceptions. Any exception requires the documented approval path and cannot be created by the knowledge assistant.
