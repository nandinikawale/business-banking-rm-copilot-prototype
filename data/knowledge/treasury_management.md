# Fictional Treasury Management Discovery Guide

This guide is invented for learning and does not describe a real bank's services or controls.

## Discovery questions

The RM asks how the business receives funds, initiates payments, controls user access, reconciles accounts, experiences fraud attempts, and forecasts cash. The discussion should identify operational pain points before naming a solution. Transaction volume alone does not establish suitability.

## ACH services

Before discussing ACH origination, review the customer's payment purpose, expected file and transaction volumes, counterparties, authorization practices, user roles, exposure needs, return history when available, and fraud controls. A treasury specialist completes product-specific assessment, documentation, limits, testing, and implementation.

## Merchant and receivables services

Merchant or receivables discussions consider sales channels, payment types, settlement timing, refunds, chargebacks, reconciliation, and information-security practices. The RM should avoid quoting unapproved pricing or representing that implementation is guaranteed.

## Implementation boundaries

The knowledge assistant may surface discovery topics and fictional procedures. It cannot establish limits, approve enrollment, modify access, or initiate a transaction. Authorized operations and treasury teams complete verification and implementation.
