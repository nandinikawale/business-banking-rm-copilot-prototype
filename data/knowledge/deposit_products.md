# Fictional Business Deposit Product Guide

This product guide is entirely fictional and contains no real pricing or eligibility rules.

## Operating checking

Business operating checking supports routine receipts and disbursements. An RM should understand expected transaction volume, average collected balance, cash handling, outgoing payments, user access, and statement needs before discussing a product. Product selection must use the current approved disclosure and fee schedule rather than this demonstration guide.

## Savings and liquidity

Business savings may support reserve funds that do not require daily movement. The RM distinguishes stable reserves from operating cash and confirms access expectations. Yield, transaction restrictions, and fees can change and must be confirmed through authorized product materials.

## Deposit movement review

A sustained decline means three consecutive month-end decreases in the fictional analytics scenario. It is a review indicator, not proof of attrition. The RM compares seasonality, known purchases, debt payments, payroll cycles, transfers, and changes in operating activity. The RM contacts the customer only after reviewing available context and must not claim that the indicator predicts intent.

A material unexplained decline is documented in the relationship review. Potential responses include a service check-in, a cash-flow discussion, or consultation with a treasury specialist. Recommendations must remain suitable for the customer's stated needs.
